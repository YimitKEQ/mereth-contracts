The Guide

How Mereth actually works, from installing it to holding a rank. Every rule below is either quoted from the client the launcher ships or cited to a dated patch note.

Getting in

You need Skyrim Special Edition, SKSE, and their launcher. The launcher reads a manifest, installs 167 Nexus mods in a fixed order, and checks 142 files against the server before you connect.

Login is through Discord

Not a username and password. The client has distinct failures for being outside their Discord and for never having logged in through it, so membership is the gate. It also fails you for a ban, an expired session, an expired token, or a different IP.

Your mod list is checked, not trusted

Plugin names, order and light versus regular flags must match, and so must the SKSE DLLs by name, size and checksum.

Having a file in Data is not enough, it must be enabled and loaded.

Skills and memory points

The thing to understand before anything else: tiers are caps you buy, not levels you grind. At character creation you get 18 memory points and spend them to set the highest tier each skill can ever reach. Novice costs 1, Apprentice 2, Adept 3, Expert 5, Master 8. Eighteen does not go far when one Master costs eight.

You allocate in the starting room or at any temple in the province. After that, experience carries you up towards the cap you bought: each tier is nineteen levels, and the twentieth advances you, if your cap allows. Experience only comes from skills you have locked in, and repeating the same action pays less over time.

Take a memory point back out and you lose the experience for the tier you dropped out of, though only if you had any in it. The client enforces the plan hard: it refuses to let you pick a lock or a pocket at all until the skill is assigned.

Assign Lockpicking in your skill plan at a temple before you can pick locks.
Lock In your skill plan before you can pickpocket. Use the Skills menu (K).

A plan reaches tier 5, Master. Legendary sits above it at level 100 and cannot be bought with memory points at all. Perks unlock every ten levels on skills that grant them.

The purple bar is your experience budget

Exhaustion drops every time you use a skill and gain experience, and only starts refilling once it is fully spent. Resting at an inn refills it noticeably faster, which is the real reason to go indoors.

No points, no damage

If you have no points in a weapon skill you deal no damage at all with that weapon. This is deliberate rather than a penalty curve: civilians should not pick up a sword and fight like a warrior, and specialists should feel like specialists.

Every skill, every tier

51 skills across 8 categories, and Mereth documents each one itself. The text under every skill is theirs, from the skill menu: five tiers saying exactly what that level lets you do. Categories are Gathering, Crafting & trades, Weapon Specializations, Armor Specializations, Magic schools, Movement & stealth, Combat styles, Performance.

Miningextracts ore, gems and minerals
  1. NoviceYou are no star-gazer, but you might dig up a few chunks of iron ore if you are very lucky. Quicksilver or corundum are clearly beyond you.
  2. ApprenticeYou have learned to handle a pick, not master it, but well enough. You are decent at mining iron, the most common metal.
  3. AdeptYou have made your mark in the mining trade. You can now mine corundum and quicksilver, and you know the first rule of orichalcum: avoid the Orcs.
  4. ExpertYou can tell moonstone from other minerals. Rare metals like gold and silver require knowledge, and you have it.
  5. MasterYour beard has grown fuller and your collar higher. Any stone in a backwater gully speaks to you; you know how to wrest something valuable from it, malachite, perhaps even ebony.
Fishingcatches fish
  1. NoviceYou know there is such a thing as a rod and that you can put bait on the hook. You even know you must cast and watch the float, but does that guarantee a catch? Hardly.
  2. ApprenticeYou have learned the basics of fishing and now know that river fish bite best on bee or lunar moth wing.
  3. AdeptThanks to long trips and hours at the line, you have found that lake fish love dragonfly most, and dungeon fish will strike at any bait.
  4. ExpertYou are an experienced fisher. You know saltwater fish bite well on blue butterfly and monarch, and that predatory fish in any water go for fighting fish.
  5. MasterYou fish like a master. Many think you are just lucky, but you know the right spot, the right bait, and a steady hand matter far more.

Parcels are the property system

A hold divides into parcels, and a parcel is the closest thing Mereth has to owning something. They are named, granted to citizens, and set to one access mode. Doors and containers bind to a parcel as fixtures, and holders carry parcel keys.

  • All citizens

  • Named residents
  • Invite only
  • Unlocked

A parcel can also demand a minimum rank, and access can be granted to people outside the hold entirely.

Trading face to face

E on another player opens a two sided trade window. Both put items and gold in, both mark ready, and it completes only when both confirm. Nothing moves on one person's word.

The calendar

World time is synced for everyone and runs on the Tamriel year: Morning Star, Sun's Dawn, First Seed, Rain's Hand, Second Seed, Midyear, Sun's Height, Last Seed, Hearthfire, Frostfall, Sun's Dusk, Evening Star.

Trouble

No bounty system and no guards who arrest you. What exists is a set of interactions players use on each other: tackle, cuff, shackle, or put your own hands up. Cuffing produces a real incapacitated state, the same as being paralysed.
