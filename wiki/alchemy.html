How this works

An ingredient carries up to four effects. Brewing two together produces a potion with every effect they share, and nothing else, which is why a pairing with no overlap wastes both.

Knowing an effect is separate from having the ingredient. Mereth tracks which effects you have learned, so this table tells you what a pairing would do, not what your character already knows it would do.
