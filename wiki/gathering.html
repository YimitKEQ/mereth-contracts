Where to gather

Every ore vein, clay pit, salt deposit, fishing spot and crafting station on Mereth, read from all 130 plugins their server loads, including the two the team writes themselves. 1,455 stand outdoors and are on the map; the rest are underground and listed beneath it.

This is Mereth's world, not vanilla Skyrim's. It is built from every plugin their server loads, including Core.esp and CorePatches.esp, which the team writes themselves and ships through their own data repository rather than Nexus. Where two plugins edit the same object the later one wins, exactly as the game resolves it, and anything a winning edit deletes or switches off is taken off the map. That removed 16,655 objects.

It checks out against their own patch notes. They wrote about clearing out broken tanning racks: the count falls from 170 in plain Skyrim to 23 here. They wrote about deleting ebony ore lying in the open: 48 falls to 29. They also quietly added four iron veins. None of that would show without their two plugins, which is how you can tell they are really in here.

The map

Drag nothing, click nothing. Hover a dot to see what it is, and use the legend to show one profession at a time. The landmass underneath is not a picture of Skyrim: it is drawn from where roughly a million objects sit, so the province draws its own outline.

What this still misses

Anything the server changes while it is running. The gamemode can place, move and delete objects at runtime, and none of that lives in a plugin file where it could be read. The evidence suggests this is a small gap rather than a large one, because the world edits their patch notes describe do turn up in their plugins, but small is not none.

Herbalism and most farming. Skyrim stores flowers, bushes and many crops as tree records rather than as flora, and the parser behind this page does not read that type. Mushrooms and a few others come through; mountain flowers and lavender do not. That is a gap in the tooling, not in the world.

Respawning. A vein is a place, not a schedule. Mereth also seals and regenerates dungeons on a timer, so what is in a mine today is not what was there last week.

69 of the 130 plugins place nothing at all and are absent for that reason alone: hair, beards, body paints, face masks, textures, the interface and the animation framework. 61 plugins put objects in the world, and all 61 are here.
