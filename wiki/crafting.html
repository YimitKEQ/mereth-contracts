Every recipe

7,033 crafting recipes, read out of the plugins Mereth loads including their own. Search by what you want to make, or by something you are holding, and it will find every recipe that consumes it. Every item carries its form id, so you can search by id and copy a ready made player.additem line.

Search recipes

By bench

Click a bench to list everything made there.

Spawning an item

Click any id in the table and the whole console line is copied. Press ~ in game, paste, press enter. 

player.additem FE022856 1

The first digits are a position, not part of the item. An id is the plugin's slot in the load order followed by the item's own id inside that plugin. A full plugin gets two digits and six of item, an ESL gets FE, three digits of slot and only three of item. The ids here are Mereth's load order, which is the one that matters on Mereth. Move the same mod into a different list and the leading digits change while the trailing ones never do, so the plugin name shown under each id is the half that always travels.

1,504 of 4,416 items are base game and their ids are identical in every install. The other 2,912 come from mods or Creation Club content, so on your own load order look the id up in game rather than trusting the leading digits: 

help "Trader's Resource" 4

That lists every match with the id your game actually assigned. Page Up and Page Down scroll the console. The 4 restricts it to items, which is the difference between four results and four hundred.

How to read this

Perks are Skyrim's, not Mereth's. Where a recipe lists a perk it is the vanilla condition baked into the record. Mereth gates crafting by its own skill tiers on top of that, so a recipe showing no perk is not necessarily one you can make.

Names repeat, ids do not. Four separate armours ship as Trader's Resource, one per colour, and nothing in game tells them apart. The id is the only way to ask for the one you meant.

497 house building recipes are set aside rather than shown. They are Hearthfire wall and furniture pieces, they outnumber everything else, and nobody searches for them.

Overrides are resolved. Where two plugins define the same recipe the later one wins, and a recipe another plugin deletes is gone. That is what makes this Mereth's recipe list rather than the sum of every mod's.
